# Face Photo Attribution

The photos in this folder are used as **illustrative stock placeholders only**.
They do not depict real study participants, and are not connected to any
camera, sensor, or facial-analysis model in this repository — the app simply
picks one at random when you click "Simulate Data."

**Source:** [Random User Generator](https://randomuser.me/) portrait library
(`https://randomuser.me/api/portraits/...`), hand-picked from the authorized
section of [UI Faces](https://uifaces.co/), originally drawn from
Greg Peverill-Conti's ["1,000 Faces" project](https://www.flickr.com/photos/gregpc/sets/72157625093827617/).
